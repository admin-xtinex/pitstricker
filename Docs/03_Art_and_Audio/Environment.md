# Pit Striker — Environment Specifications

## 1. Arena Variations

### Theme A: Sunny Shore (Beach Sand)
* **Floor Texture:** Fine compacted golden sand with gentle wave ripple normal maps.
* **Border Rail:** Weathered driftwood logs acting as bouncy boundary walls.
* **Pits:** Natural shell-lined depressions in the sand.
* **Atmosphere:** Warm golden sunlight, soft breezy ambient dust motes.

### Theme B: Courtyard (Hard Baked Clay)
* **Floor Texture:** Terracotta tile or smooth packed red clay.
* **Border Rail:** Chiseled stone border with chipped corners.
* **Pits:** Carved circular stone sockets.
* **Atmosphere:** Midday crisp lighting with sharp contact shadows.

### Theme C: Garden Soil (Rough Earth)
* **Floor Texture:** Dark rich topsoil with scattered micro-pebbles.
* **Border Rail:** Bent birch branches or brass garden edging.
* **Pits:** Hand-dug earth hollows with tiny root textures.
* **Atmosphere:** Overcast soft diffused lighting.
