# Pit Striker — Asset Inventory & Tracking

*This document registers all third-party and custom assets imported into the project, ensuring license compliance and file provenance.*

## 1. Asset Classification Rules
* **Custom Assets:** Created in-house. Placed under `Unity/Assets/_Project/`.
* **Third-Party Assets:** Purchased from Asset Store or open source. Placed exclusively in `Unity/Assets/ThirdParty/`.
* **Prototyping / Temporary Assets:** Must be prefixed with `TEMP_` and listed in the table below.
