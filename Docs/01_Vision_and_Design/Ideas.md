# Pit Striker — Brainstorming & Ideas Backlog

*Use this document as an unconstrained scratchpad for creative thoughts. Never discard an idea; store it here for review during design cycles.*

* **Power Strikes:** A timed tap mini-game while pulling back to grant an extra 10% "sweet-spot" speed boost.
* **Surface Variation Zones:** A patch of sticky mud in the corner that stops marbles instantly.
* **Marble Size Classes:**
  * Heavy Marble (Shooter): High mass, pushes other marbles easily, hard to sink.
  * Light Marble (Jack/Target): Low mass, scatters rapidly when bumped.
* **Spectator Mode:** Top-down broadcast camera with real-time replay angles for game-winning shots.
* **Daily Trick Shot:** A single daily puzzle shared globally with a leader board based on stroke accuracy.
