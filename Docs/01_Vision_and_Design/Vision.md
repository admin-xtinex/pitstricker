# Pit Striker — Game Vision

## 1. High Concept
*Pit Striker* is a physics-driven, turn-based marble tactical game designed for Android. Combining the spatial satisfaction of billiards, the traditional charm of outdoor ground-marble games, and the strategic positioning of curling, players flick marbles across tactile terrain (beach sand, hard clay, garden soil) to sink targets into pits while knocking opponents out of scoring position.

## 2. Emotional Pillars
1. **Tactile Weight & Kinetic Satisfaction:** Marbles must feel heavy, dense, and solid. Impacts produce crisp acoustic clacks and subtle device vibrations.
2. **Easy to Learn, Deep to Master:** Anyone can drag and release to shoot, but banking shots off terrain curves and executing carom knockouts reward skill.
3. **High Table-Top Tension:** Every millimeter matters. Watching a marble teeter on the precipice of a pit creates edge-of-seat drama.

## 3. Platform & Target Specs
* **Primary Platform:** Android 8.0 (API Level 26) and above.
* **Target Frame Rate:** Stable 60 FPS across mid-tier and budget devices.
* **Orientation:** Portrait (one-thumb playable) or Landscape (flexible review in Phase 3).
* **Network Strategy:** Phase 0–5 is 100% offline / local pass-and-play. Online multiplayer designed cleanly for future expansion without refactoring core simulation.

## 4. What Pit Striker Is NOT (Scope Boundaries)
* **NOT a high-speed arcade rolling game (like Super Monkey Ball):** Marbles are propelled via discrete strikes, not tilt control.
* **NOT an RPG / Hero collector:** No stat-grinding, loot-boxes, or pay-to-win physics upgrades.
* **NOT a hyper-casual ad-farm:** High-quality presentation, clean aesthetics, and fair monetization.
