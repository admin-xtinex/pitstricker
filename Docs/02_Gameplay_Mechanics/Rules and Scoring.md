# Pit Striker — Rules and Scoring System

## 1. Match Structure
* **Players:** 2 to 4 players (Pass-and-Play locally on a single device).
* **Marbles:** 1 Striker per player + 5 Target (Neutral) Marbles positioned in arena formation.

## 2. Round Flow & Turn Rules
1. **The Break:** Player 1 executes the opening strike from the baseline toward the center formation.
2. **Standard Turn:** Active player has 1 strike attempt.
3. **Turn Bonus ("Continue Turn"):** If a player legally sinks a neutral marble or an opponent's marble into a pit, they receive **one additional strike**. Max consecutive bonus strikes: 2 (to avoid runaway turns).
4. **Fouls:**
   * **Scratch (Striker Sunk):** If the player's own striker falls into any pit, player forfeits turn and 1 point is deducted. The striker is respawned at the baseline.
   * **Out of Arena:** If any marble flies over the boundary wall, it is placed at the center of the ring.

## 3. Scoring Matrix

| Action | Points Awarded | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Sink Neutral Marble (Outer Pit) | +10 pts | Marble removed from board. |
| Sink Neutral Marble (Center Pit) | +25 pts | Center pit has tighter lip. |
| Sink Opponent Striker (Any Pit) | +15 pts | Opponent respawns at baseline next turn. |
| Direct Bank Shot Sink (Off Wall) | +5 bonus pts | Multiplier applied to base sink score. |
| Scratch / Self-Sink (Foul) | -10 pts | Striker respawned at baseline. |
