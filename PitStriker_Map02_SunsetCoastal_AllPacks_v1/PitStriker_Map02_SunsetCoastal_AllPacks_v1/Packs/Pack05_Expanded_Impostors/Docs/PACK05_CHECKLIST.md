## Intended use
These assets are designed for:
- Camera A / Camera B controlled scenic composition
- transparent quads / cards
- layered parallax
- distant environment illusion
- reducing full-3D environment cost

## Unity guidance
- Use Unlit or very simple Lit materials for very distant cards.
- Disable colliders on all Pack 05 assets.
- Disable real-time shadows for far cards.
- Use material batching / atlas where possible.
- Do not place these so close that flatness becomes obvious.
- Test both camera directions and the Camera A↔B blend corridor.
